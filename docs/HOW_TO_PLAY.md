# How to Play

This page explains the goal, the rules and the controls of the game. It also lists the five Pokémon and their moves, and ends with a few tips.

## The goal

Each player has a team of 3 Pokémon. The player and the computer take turns attacking each other. The first side to make all 3 enemy Pokémon faint wins.

## The basics in one minute

- Every Pokémon starts with 10 HP (health points). At 0 HP it faints.
- Each round, both sides roll 8 dice. Dice work like coins: they are spent to attack, play cards or switch Pokémon.
- Only one Pokémon per side fights at a time. It is called the active Pokémon. The other two wait on the bench.
- Sides take turns doing one action each. When both sides have stopped, the round ends and a new one begins.

## Starting a match

1. Click **BEGIN** on the title screen.
2. Pick 3 of the 5 Pokémon. The numbers under the cards show the order. The first one picked starts as the active Pokémon.
3. Click **BEGIN DUEL**. The computer gets 3 random Pokémon.

## The Pokémon

Each Pokémon has an element and three moves. The moves get stronger from left to right.

| Pokémon | Element | Basic (2 damage) | Skill (3 damage) | Ultimate (5 damage) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pikachu | Electric | Quick Attack | Thunderbolt | Volt Tackle |
| Charizard | Fire | Ember | Flamethrower | Blast Burn |
| Blastoise | Water | Water Gun | Hydro Pump | Hydro Cannon |
| Venusaur | Grass | Vine Whip | Solar Beam | Frenzy Plant |
| Mewtwo | Psychic | Confusion | Psystrike | Psycho Boost |

## Dice

At the start of every round, each side rolls 8 new dice. Each die has one of six types: Electric, Fire, Water, Grass, Psychic or Omni.

- About half of the dice match the elements in the player's own team, so useful dice come up often.
- An **Omni** die counts as any element.
- Dice that are not spent are thrown away when the round ends, so it is best to use them.

## Moves and their cost

| Move | Cost | Damage |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 1 die of the Pokémon's element, plus 2 dice of any type | 2 |
| Skill | 3 dice of the Pokémon's element | 3 |
| Ultimate | 3 dice of the Pokémon's element, plus full energy | 5 |

Omni dice can stand in for the Pokémon's element.

### Energy

Each Pokémon has 3 energy dots under its card. Basic and Skill attacks add 1 energy. The Ultimate needs all 3 dots and empties them again.

## Reactions

When a Pokémon is hit, it gets the attacker's element as an **aura**, shown as a small die on the card. Think of it as a sticker that marks the last element that hit it.

If the Pokémon is then hit by a **different** element, a reaction happens. The attack does **2 extra damage**, and the aura is removed. If the same element hits again, nothing special happens.

| Elements that meet | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Fire and Electric | Overload |
| Fire and Water | Vaporize |
| Water and Electric | Electro-Charged |
| Grass and Fire | Burning |
| Grass and Water | Bloom |
| Grass and Electric | Quicken |
| Any pair that includes Psychic | Confusion |

Every reaction gives the same bonus. The names are only for fun.

## Support cards

At the start of the first round each side draws 5 cards. After that, 2 cards are drawn each round. A hand holds up to 7 cards. Playing a card costs dice of any type and does **not** end the turn.

| Card | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Potion | 1 die | Heals the active Pokémon for 3 HP (never above 10) |
| Rare Candy | 1 die | The active Pokémon gains 1 energy |
| Choice Band | 2 dice | The next attack deals 2 extra damage |
| Poké Ball | 1 die | Draw 2 more cards |

## Switching Pokémon

Click a Pokémon on the bench to make it the active one. This costs 1 die of any type and uses up the turn. A fainted Pokémon cannot be chosen.

When the active Pokémon faints, the next living one is sent out automatically.

## Turns and rounds

- In round 1, the player goes first.
- In later rounds, whoever ended the previous round first goes first.
- Attacking and switching end the turn. Playing a card does not.
- **End Round** means a player stops acting for the rest of the round. If one side has ended its round, the other side keeps taking turns until it ends too.
- When both sides have ended the round, new dice are rolled, new cards are drawn and the next round starts.

## Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Attack | Click a move button on the right |
| Play a card | Click a card in the hand at the bottom |
| Switch Pokémon | Click a Pokémon on the bench |
| End the round | Click **End Round** |

A few hints on the screen:

- Moving the mouse over a move or card lifts the dice that would be spent.
- A dimmed button or card means the cost cannot be paid right now.
- If a cost cannot be paid, the message "Not enough dice!" appears.
- The battle log in the middle shows the last few actions.

## The battle screen

- **Top:** the computer's team. Its cards in hand and its dice count are shown at the top left.
- **Bottom:** the player's team and the hand of support cards.
- **Left:** the player's dice.
- **Right:** the round and turn panel, and the move buttons for the active Pokémon.

## Winning and losing

The match ends when one side has no Pokémon left. The screen shows **VICTORY** or **DEFEAT**. Click **PLAY AGAIN** to go back to team select.

## Tips

- Pick Pokémon of different elements. Reactions need two different elements to meet.
- A Basic attack followed by a different element often does 4 damage instead of 2.
- Play Choice Band just before a big attack, like an Ultimate with a reaction (5 + 2 + 2 = 9 damage).
- Spend dice before ending the round, because unused dice are lost.
- Use Rare Candy to reach full energy sooner.
